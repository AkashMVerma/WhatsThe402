How the web answers AI agents

When an AI agent fetches a page for a person, the site answers in one of three ways:

  • 200Serve it. The agent gets the page.
  • 403Refuse it. Forbidden: the site blocks the agent.
  • 402Ask for payment. Payment Required: the page has a price.

This index tracks refusals and payment requests across Cloudflare's network, month by month and industry by industry.

Industries

Two measures answer two different questions. Rate is how often an industry refuses or charges the agents that visit it. Share of total is where all those responses come from. A small industry can have a high rate and still a small share.
